Bootcamp · Ramp 2, south end

Bondi Beach

How to find us
We train at the south end of Bondi Beach, in front of Ramp 2, which is next to the skate park (opposite Hall Street). Look for the Team One flag.
Meeting point
6:00am class: meet in the beach car park opposite the training spot. 6:45am class: meet at the training spot on the beach.
Rainy days
We move onto the promenade or under one of the huts on the grass area, so we can train rain, hail or shine.
Class times
Tuesday 6:00am and 6:45am · Saturday 7:15am and 8:00am
Bring
A bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.

Bootcamp · North end, by the volleyball courts

Coogee Beach

How to find us
We train at the north end of Coogee Beach, next to the beach volleyball courts. From the Dolphin Street car park, walk down the small white stairs. Look for the Team One flag.
Meeting point
6:00am class: meet in the Dolphin Street car park near the Pavilion. 6:45am class: meet at the training spot on the beach.
Rainy days
We move undercover to the huts with the BBQs next to the beach car park, opposite the Pavilion.
Class times
Monday 6:00am and 6:45am · Friday 6:00am and 6:45am
Bring
A bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.
