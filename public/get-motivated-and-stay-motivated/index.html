Get motivated and stay motivated

By Yves Minten Posted 30 September 2016 2 min read

Spring has definitely started, daylight savings is about to start and after having the winter blues, many people want to get motivated, fit and healthy for summer. Getting out of your winter blues and back into a training routine is often easier said than done. Here a few things you can do to get motivated and increase your success rate of getting and staying in a healthy routine.

Have a plan


Make an exercise plan make the necessary bookings or arrangements because this will help you to stick to your plan. Booking in for a session means that you've made a commitment ahead of the session and you’ll be less likely to change your plan or come up with excuses not to go.

Try group training


Group training with like-minded people with the same or similar training goals works as a great motivator. Knowing that it is not just you who is struggling to get through a workout routine can help you push yourself to your limits. Having other people around you might make the difference between quitting when you’re tired and pushing yourself a bit more!

Challenge yourself


To stay motivated it is important to keep giving yourself challenges. These challenges or goals can be big or small. It’s a good idea to start with the bigger challenges and then to break these down into smaller once. Think about what you need to do reach your bigger goals. For example, to be able to run 15km’s without stopping in 3 months, you might need to do 2 running sessions per week and run 5km in the first week.

Work out with a buddy


Training with a buddy can be very motivating. If you’ve made arrangements with your workout buddy to go to a class then the last thing you want to do is let them down. A workout buddy with a similar fitness level can push you so you reach your goals quicker.

Eat the right nutrition


Eating the right nutrition is important when you want to lead an active lifestyle. Give your body the nutrition it needs to function properly. Remember, often nutrition and exercise go hand in hand. Having a healthy diet can motivate you to start exercising and on the other hand doing an Amazing workout can motivate you to have a clean balanced diet.

Be proud of yourself and enjoy!


Get motivated and be proud of it. Be proud of yourself for being motivated and active. Embrace change and enjoy the amazing benefits of exercising. Enjoy the endorphins after a great workout and the progress that you’re making.

A new season is a great time to start a new routine and these easy, motivating ideas will help you get started. Soon, you’ll become a healthier and happier you for the new season ahead.
