Getting started

New to Team One?

Do I have to be fit to join Team One Fitness sessions?

Not at all. Our sessions are designed so that everyone can work at their own pace and level, whatever their fitness. We'll show you how to make each exercise easier or harder.

How do I start?

Start with our 7-day trial for $15. You can come to as many classes as you like for a week, with no lock-in contract.

How do I book a class?

Booking is essential. The easiest way is the Mindbody app (search for Team One Fitness), or use the live timetable on our website.

How long is a class?

Every bootcamp class is 45 minutes.

At the beach

On the day.

What do I have to bring to the session?

Bring comfortable workout gear and a bottle of water. Beach sessions are normally a barefoot affair, but on rainy days or in winter, bring shoes. In sunny weather we recommend sunscreen and a hat. We provide all the equipment.

Where do we meet and train?

Bondi: on the sand at the south end of the beach, in front of Ramp 2 next to the skate park. Coogee: on the sand at the north end of the beach, next to the volleyball courts, down the small white stairs from the Dolphin Street car park. Look for the Team One flag. See maps and directions.

Will sessions be cancelled due to bad weather?

We train rain, hail or shine! On rainy days we move to a sheltered spot nearby: the promenade or the huts on the grass at Bondi, and the BBQ huts by the beach car park at Coogee. See where we train. In extreme weather we cancel the session and let you know by email.

Can I train at both Bondi and Coogee?

Yes. Every bootcamp membership and class pack lets you train at all our locations, so you can mix Bondi and Coogee classes in the same week.

Should I eat something before the training session?

It depends on how much time you have. If you train first thing in the morning, a small, easy snack such as a banana or a few sips of a smoothie is plenty, or nothing at all if you prefer. If you have 1 to 2 hours, try something light like yoghurt with fruit or cereal with milk. With 3 to 4 hours, a normal meal such as baked beans on toast or pasta with a tomato sauce works well. Avoid heavy, fatty food just before training.

How often do you run the Shred?

We run our Shred programs, the 4 Week Shred and the Rapid Shred, 2 or 3 times a year. Ask us when the next one starts.

Personal training

One-on-one with a coach.

How much does a personal training session cost?

One-on-one sessions start from $110 for a one-hour session. Training with a friend or in a small group of up to 4 people costs a little more in total but less per person. Get in touch and we'll give you a price for your situation.

Where can I do personal training?

Wherever suits you best: our home gym in Botany, on the beach or in a park at Bondi or Coogee, or at your home in the Eastern Suburbs. See all personal training locations.

How long is a session?

Sessions are one hour, unless you'd like something different.

Can I train with a friend?

Yes. Buddy and small-group sessions are available for up to 4 people, which makes each session cheaper per person.

What if I need to cancel a session?

We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Please let us know at least 24 hours before your session. Sessions cancelled less than 24 hours before the start time are forfeited.

Memberships

Memberships and payments.

What membership options are there after the trial?

Choose a flexible weekly membership (2 classes, 3 classes or unlimited classes a week) or a casual class pack (1, 10 or 20 sessions). See prices.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Weekly memberships are week to week. There are no joining fees, no cancellation fees and no hidden costs.

How do I cancel or pause my weekly membership?

Email info@teamonefitness.com.au by the Tuesday before your next payment and we'll cancel or pause it.

How long do I have to use my class pack?

A 10-class pack is valid for 4 months, and a 20-class pack for 6 months, from the day you buy it.

Do unused weekly classes carry over?

No. Your weekly membership gives you a set number of classes each week, and unused classes don't carry over to the next week.

When is my weekly membership paid?

Weekly memberships are paid by direct debit on Monday morning.

Can I get a refund?

Memberships are non-refundable. If you can't train for a while, you can pause your weekly membership instead.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. Bring a friend and get 2 free classes when they join. See how it works.
