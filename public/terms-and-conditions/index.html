Terms and conditions

For bootcamp memberships, class packs and personal training.

Bootcamp

Outdoor group training

  1. The $15 7-day trial is for new members only, one trial per person.
  2. Weekly memberships are ongoing and are paid by direct debit every Monday. To cancel, pause or change your membership, email info@teamonefitness.com.au at least 7 days before your next payment day.
  3. If our prices change, we'll give members at least one week's notice.
  4. Our cancellation window is 30 minutes before the start of a class. Our booking window is 14 days up to 30 minutes before the start of the class.
  5. Sessions still occur in bad weather, but we reserve the right at our discretion to cancel any session for unforeseen circumstances, including but not limited to extreme bad weather or public holidays. These cancelled sessions will be forfeited.
  6. Due to the number of days in a month, bad weather and public holidays, some weeks or months will have more or fewer sessions than others. Regardless of the number of sessions, the agreed weekly fee will be charged.
  7. Missed sessions are forfeited unless there is an agreed upon extended client absence.
  8. Casual 10-session packs need to be used within 4 months and casual 20-session packs within 6 months.
  9. Memberships and class packs are non-refundable, and class packs can't be transferred to another person. If you can't train for a while, you can pause your weekly membership instead.

Personal training

Personal training

  1. Sessions still go ahead in bad weather.
  2. We have a 24-hour cancellation policy. Sessions cancelled less than 24 hours before the start time are forfeited.
  3. If you cancel at least 24 hours before, your session will be rescheduled within 2 weeks.
  4. If we have to cancel a session because of bad weather, a public holiday or anything unforeseen, we'll reschedule it with you.
  5. Personal training is paid by direct debit every Monday. To pause or stop your sessions, email info@teamonefitness.com.au at least 7 days before your next payment day.
