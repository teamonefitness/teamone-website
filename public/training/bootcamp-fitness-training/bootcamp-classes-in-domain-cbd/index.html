Why train with Team One?

Great team spirit.

At Team One we enjoy great team spirit, with many members having trained with us for over 10 years. Our members share in their successes and support each other to strive towards their individual training goals. This can make the difference between cancelling a session at the last minute, or feeling motivated to get down to meet your training buddies and blast out a great workout!

Team One group training lets members reap the benefits of fun, motivating and high-intensity bootcamp-style training, with the same focus on safety, technique and achievement that you would receive in a one-to-one personal training session.

Participating in a group training environment gives you the added benefit of motivation from your peers as well as your coach. Group training brings energy, inspiration and a lot of fun whilst keeping active.

Type of training

What a Team One class looks like.

Every class is 45 minutes, different every time, and suitable for all fitness levels. Your coach adjusts the exercises and intensity to suit you.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises. Plenty of variety and a great full-body workout.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. You'll get stronger and build lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.
