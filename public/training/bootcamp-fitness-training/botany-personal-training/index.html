Personal trainer in Botany

Extra support to reach your goals faster.

Looking for a personal trainer in Botany, Mascot, Pagewood, Eastgardens or Maroubra? Personal training gives you a program built around you, a coach who keeps you accountable, and faster results than training on your own. We'll help you:

  • Improve your fitness, lose weight, tone up or build muscle

  • Stay active during pregnancy or return to training after giving birth
  • Build confidence before joining our group classes

How we train

Strength and cardio, built around you.

Sessions combine strength and cardio using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls, battle ropes, boxing and high-intensity circuits. Prefer running, or want to focus on strength? We'll tailor every session to your goals.

Every session is one hour, unless you'd like something different. Want to train with a friend? Small-group sessions are available for up to 4 people, so you share the cost.

Meet your trainer
