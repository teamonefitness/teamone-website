Coogee Beach group training

Train at sunrise on Coogee Beach.

Join our outdoor fitness classes on Coogee Beach and train with a friendly group on Monday and Friday mornings before work. Every class is 45 minutes, coached from start to finish, and different every time.

We train on the sand at the north end of Coogee Beach, rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move undercover to the huts with the BBQs next to the beach car park, opposite the Pavilion, so the class always goes ahead.

All fitness levels are welcome. We'll show you how to make each exercise easier or harder.

Where to find us

North end, near the volleyball courts.

We train on the sand at the north end of Coogee Beach, near the volleyball courts. Look for the Team One flag. Arrive a few minutes early for your first class and say hi to the coach.

Bring: a bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.

Type of training

What a Coogee class looks like.

Every class is 45 minutes, different every time, and suitable for all fitness levels. Your coach adjusts the exercises and intensity to suit you.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises. Plenty of variety and a great full-body workout.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. You'll get stronger and build lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.

Questions

Coogee Beach bootcamp FAQs.

Are the Coogee Beach bootcamp classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our outdoor fitness classes are for all fitness levels. We'll show you how to make each exercise easier or harder, so you can train at your own level.

How long is a class?

Every bootcamp class is 45 minutes, so you can train before work and still be on time.

What happens when it rains?

We train rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move undercover to the huts with the BBQs next to the Coogee Beach car park, opposite the Pavilion.

How do I start?

Start with our 7-day trial for $15. You can come to as many classes as you like for a week, with no lock-in contract.

What membership options are there after the trial?

If you'd like to keep training with us, choose a flexible weekly membership (2 classes, 3 classes or unlimited classes a week) or a casual class pack (1, 10 or 20 sessions). Can I also train at Bondi Beach?

Yes. All memberships let you train at any of our locations, including our Bondi Beach bootcamp.

Is this like a gym in Coogee?

Yes, but outdoors, with a coach and a group. Instead of training alone, you get a coached 45-minute strength and fitness workout on the sand with kettlebells, dumbbells, ropes and more.
