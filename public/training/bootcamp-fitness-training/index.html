How it works

Coached from start to finish.

Every class is 45 minutes, different every time, and suitable for all fitness levels. Your coach shows you how to make each exercise easier or harder, so you can train at your own level.

We train rain, hail or shine, so you can count on your class going ahead.

Whether you want to get fit for summer, keep in shape through winter, build lean muscle, improve your cardio or meet new people, our bootcamps are a great way to do it.

Type of training

Two class styles.

You'll see these on the timetable. Both use kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls, ropes and more.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises. Plenty of variety and a great full-body workout.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. You'll get stronger and build lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.

Questions

Bootcamp FAQs.

Are the bootcamp classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our classes are for all fitness levels. Your coach shows you how to make each exercise easier or harder, so you can train at your own level.

How long is a class?

Every bootcamp class is 45 minutes, so you can train before work and still be on time.

Can I train at both Bondi and Coogee?

Yes. One membership covers both beaches, so you can mix and match your classes across the week.

What happens when it rains?

We train rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move to the promenade or a sheltered spot nearby. See the rainy-day spot for each beach →

How do I start?

Start with our 7-day trial for $15. You can come to as many classes as you like for a week, with no lock-in contract.
