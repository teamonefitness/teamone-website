Bondi Beach group training

Kick-start your day on the sand.

Join our Bondi Beach bootcamp and train with a friendly group on Tuesday before work or on Saturday morning. Every class is 45 minutes, coached from start to finish, and different every time.

We train on the sand at Bondi Beach, rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move onto the promenade or under one of the huts on the grass area, so the class always goes ahead.

Whether you're new to exercise or already fit, we'll adjust every exercise to suit you.

Where to find us

On the sand opposite Hall St.

Look for the orange Team One flag on the sand at Bondi Beach, opposite Hall Street. Arrive a few minutes early for your first class and say hi to the coach.

Bring: a water bottle and a towel. Most people train barefoot on the sand. We supply all the equipment.

Type of training

What a Bondi class looks like.

Every class is 45 minutes, different every time, and suitable for all fitness levels. Your coach adjusts the exercises and intensity to suit you.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises. Plenty of variety and a great full-body workout.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. You'll get stronger and build lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.
