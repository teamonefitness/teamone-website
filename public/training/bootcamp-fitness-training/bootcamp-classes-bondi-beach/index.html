Bondi Beach group training

Kick-start your day on the sand.

Join our outdoor group fitness classes on Bondi Beach and train with a friendly group on Tuesday before work or on Saturday morning. Every class is 45 minutes, coached from start to finish, and different every time.

We train on the sand at Bondi Beach, rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move onto the promenade or under one of the huts on the grass area.

All fitness levels are welcome. We'll show you how to make each exercise easier or harder.

Where to find us

Ramp 2, south end of Bondi Beach.

We train on the sand at Ramp 2, at the south end of Bondi Beach. Look for the Team One flag. Arrive a few minutes early for your first class and say hi to the coach.

Bring: a bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.

Type of training

What a Bondi class looks like.

Every class is 45 minutes, different every time, and suitable for all fitness levels. Your coach adjusts the exercises and intensity to suit you.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises. Plenty of variety and a great full-body workout.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. You'll get stronger and build lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.

Questions

Bondi Beach bootcamp FAQs.

Are the Bondi Beach bootcamp classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our group fitness classes are for all fitness levels. We'll show you how to make each exercise easier or harder, so you can train at your own level.

How long is a class?

Every bootcamp class is 45 minutes, so you can train before work and still be on time.

What happens when it rains?

We train rain, hail or shine. On rainy days we move onto the promenade or under one of the huts on the grass area at Bondi.

How do I start?

Start with our 7-day trial for $15. You can come to as many classes as you like for a week, with no lock-in contract.

What membership options are there after the trial?

If you'd like to keep training with us, choose a flexible weekly membership (2 classes, 3 classes or unlimited classes a week) or a casual class pack (1, 10 or 20 sessions). There's no lock-in contract and no joining or cancellation fees, and you can train at Bondi and Coogee. See memberships and prices →

Can I also train at Coogee Beach?

Yes. All memberships let you train at any of our locations, including our Coogee Beach bootcamp.

Is this like an outdoor gym in Bondi?

Yes, but with a coach and a group. Instead of training alone, you get a coached 45-minute workout on the sand with kettlebells, dumbbells, ropes and more.

Other ways to train with Team One.

Prefer a different beach or one-on-one coaching? All memberships let you train at any of our locations.
