Personal trainer in the Eastern Suburbs

Extra support to reach your goals faster.

Looking for a personal trainer in Coogee, Bondi, Botany or somewhere else in Sydney's Eastern Suburbs? Personal training gives you a program built around you, a coach who keeps you accountable, and faster results than training on your own.

Train with Yves, founder of Team One, or one of our experienced coaches. We'll help you:

  • Improve your fitness, lose weight or build lean muscle
  • Come back from a sports injury
  • Get in shape for your wedding
  • Stay active during pregnancy or return to training after giving birth
  • Build confidence before joining our group classes

Questions

Personal training FAQs.

How much does a personal training session cost?

One-on-one sessions start from $110 for a one-hour session. Training with a friend or in a small group of up to 4 people costs a little more in total but less per person. Get in touch and we'll give you a price for your situation.

Where can I do personal training?

Wherever suits you best:

  • Botany: our fully equipped home gym at 1481 Botany Road, or Sir Joseph Banks Park and Booralee Park nearby.
  • Coogee: on the beach, in the park next to the beach car park, or at the outdoor gym on Neptune Street.
  • Bondi: on the beach, or on the grass area at the south end of the Bondi skate park.
  • Eastern Suburbs: in parks and at clients' homes around Randwick, Bronte, Dover Heights, Centennial Park, Woollahra and Bellevue Hill.
  • City: in the Domain, next to the Art Gallery.
How long is a session?

Sessions are one hour, unless you'd like something different.

Who will be my trainer?

Most sessions are with Yves, the founder of Team One, or one of our experienced Team One coaches.

Is personal training good for beginners?

Yes. Many people start with personal training to build confidence and fitness before they join our group classes at Bondi or Coogee.

Can I train with a friend?

Yes. Buddy and small-group sessions are available for up to 4 people, which makes each session cheaper per person.

Prefer training in a group?
