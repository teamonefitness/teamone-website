Classes for your team

Choose your workout.

Pick one type of class or mix them up. Every session is coached from start to finish, and every exercise can be made easier or harder.

Hybrid

The best of strength and cardio in one workout. Functional strength training combined with high-intensity cardio and conditioning, using dumbbells, kettlebells, medicine balls, ropes, resistance bands and bodyweight exercises.

Strength & Burn

A workout built around functional compound strength exercises. Your team gets stronger and builds lean muscle using kettlebells, dumbbells, medicine balls and battle ropes.

Box Fit

Boxing drills combined with cardio and strength exercises. A fun way to build fitness, coordination and a bit of friendly competition.

Legs & Bums

A lower-body focused workout to build strength in the legs and glutes.

Stretching

A calmer session to improve flexibility and release tension. A great balance for people who sit at a desk all day.

Questions

Corporate fitness FAQs.

Where do corporate sessions take place?

Mainly in the Domain in the Sydney CBD, but we also train teams at Bondi Beach, Coogee Beach or any park in the Eastern Suburbs. Tell us where your office is and we'll suggest the best spot.

Can we book a one-off session?

Yes. We run one-off sessions, for example as a team-building event, as well as regular weekly classes for your team.

How much does corporate fitness cost?

The price depends on the length of the sessions, the number of people and how often you train. Send us an enquiry and we'll put together a quote for your team.

Is it suitable for all fitness levels?

Yes. Every exercise can be made easier or harder, so everyone in your team can train at their own level.

What kind of classes can we choose?

Hybrid, Strength & Burn, Box Fit, Legs & Bums and stretching, or team-building activities. We can mix them to suit your team.
