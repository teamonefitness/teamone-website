Banana Blueberry Smoothie

Posted 29 August 2014 1 min read Recipes Nutrition

Do you sometimes wonder what to eat or drink before or after a training session, and is time not always on your side, then this banana blueberry smoothie might be a great option.

Why is this smoothie so awesome?

  • Not many ingredients are needed
  • This snack is easy to make and won't take much of your time.
  • This smoothie is high in antioxidants
  • On top of this it will give you a great burst of natural energy.

How to make a banana blueberry smoothie?

Ingredients:

  • 1 banana (frozen is fine)
  • 1/2 cup blueberries (frozen is fine)
  • 2 tablespoons chia seeds
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut beverage

Preparation:

  • Blend all ingredients together and ENJOY!

Chia seeds absorb a lot of water and if you like the smoothie to be more liquidy then you can add some almond milk.
