The fine print

  • All bootcamp options give you access to all classes at all locations.

Questions

Membership FAQs.

Can I train at both Bondi and Coogee?

Yes. Every bootcamp membership and class pack lets you train at all our locations, so you can mix Bondi and Coogee classes in the same week.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Weekly memberships are week to week. There are no joining fees, no cancellation fees and no hidden costs.

How do I cancel or pause my weekly membership?

Email info@teamonefitness.com.au by the Tuesday before your next payment and we'll cancel or suspend it.

When is my weekly membership paid?

Weekly memberships are paid by direct debit on Monday morning.

How do I book a class?

Booking is essential. The easiest way is the Mindbody app (search for Team One Fitness), or use the live timetable on our website.

Which option should I choose?

Start with the 7-day trial for $15. If you train 2 or 3 times a week, Bronze or Silver suits you best. If you're planning on training up to 4 times a week, Gold works out cheapest per class. If your schedule changes a lot, a class pack lets you train whenever suits you.

Not sure where to start?

Try a week of classes for $15, then choose the option that suits you. Or call Yves on 0424 245 578.
