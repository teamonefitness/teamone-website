The fine print

Referral program terms & conditions

Who can refer a friend?

Current members with an active weekly membership. Your friend must not have been a bootcamp member in the last 12 months.

When do I get my 2 free weeks?

When your friend signs up for a weekly membership (2, 3 or unlimited classes a week) and stays active for at least 4 weeks in a row. The 2 free weeks are then added to your account.

How does Team One know my friend came from me?

Your friend writes your name on the Health & Waiver form when they sign up for the $15 7-day trial. Once they are registered, we'll get in touch with you.

Can I refer more than one friend?

Yes. Each friend who joins earns you another 2 free weeks.

Anything else I should know?
  • Free weeks can't be transferred or swapped for cash.
  • This offer can't be combined with other promotions.
  • Team One may change or end the program at any time. Friends who have already signed up keep the terms that applied when they joined.
