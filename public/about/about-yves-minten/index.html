About Yves Minten

Founder and head trainer of Team One. Firefighter, dad of three and lover of the outdoors.

Meet Yves

25 years of helping people get fitter.

Yves has worked in the fitness industry for the past 25 years. He loves health and fitness, and he loves helping people reach their goals. Every session is tailored to the people in front of him, whatever their age or ability.

He founded Team One in 2012 and has been coaching our members on Sydney's beaches ever since.

From Belgium to Sydney

A lifetime in fitness.

Yves grew up in Belgium, where he completed a Bachelor of Physical Education. He specialised in teaching adventure sports such as climbing, abseiling and canoeing, organised corporate team-building activities, and taught spin classes for eight years at a leading Belgian fitness centre.

He moved to Australia in 2009 and started Team One in 2012, running outdoor bootcamp classes on Sydney's beaches and in its parks, along with personal training, small-group training and corporate fitness.

Outside Team One

Firefighter, dad and Botany local.

When he isn't coaching, Yves works as a firefighter. He's also a father of three, and has lived in Botany since 2020, where he runs Team One's private home gym for personal training.

Yves loves the outdoors, from camping and hiking to canoeing, and just about any physical activity. He's also a big fan of HYROX, the indoor fitness race that combines running with functional workouts.

How Yves trains

Hybrid training: strength and cardio.

Yves coaches hybrid training: a mix of strength and cardio in every session. Workouts combine functional, full-body movements with high-intensity intervals, using a wide range of equipment so no two sessions are the same. He pairs training with practical nutrition advice to help you get results that last.

Above all, he builds a community. Many Team One members have trained with Yves for years, and the training partners you meet often become friends.
