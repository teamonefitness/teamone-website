Our story

Why I started Team One.

“I started Team One in July 2012 because I love helping people reach their goals, not just physically but mentally too. I also love bringing people together. Many friendships have started at Team One.”

Training makes you stronger, and it also clears your head and lifts your mood. That’s why we train outdoors, on the sand and in the fresh air, where you get all the benefits of being outside.

Our workouts feature a bootcamp style of training, with no sergeant major! We exercise in a safe and effective way, and we partner with you as you reach your goals.
