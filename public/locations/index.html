Where we train

Find your nearest Team One class.

Our bootcamp classes run on the sand at Bondi Beach and Coogee Beach, rain, hail or shine. One membership covers both beaches. Below you'll find exactly where we meet and where we go on rainy days.

Bondi Beach Coogee Beach Personal training

Bondi Beach bootcamp

Ramp 2, south end of Bondi Beach.

How to find us
We train at the south end of Bondi Beach, in front of Ramp 2, which is next to the skate park (opposite Hall Street). Look for the Team One flag.
Rainy days
We move onto the promenade or under one of the huts on the grass area, so the class always goes ahead.
Class times
Tuesday 6:00am and 6:45am · Saturday 7:15am and 8:00am
Bring
A bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.

Coogee Beach bootcamp

North end, near the volleyball courts.

How to find us
We train at the north end of Coogee Beach, next to the beach volleyball courts. Look for the Team One flag.
Rainy days
We move undercover to the huts with the BBQs next to the beach car park, opposite the Pavilion.
Class times
Monday 6:00am and 6:45am · Friday 6:00am and 6:45am
Bring
A bottle of water. On rainy days or in winter, bring shoes.
